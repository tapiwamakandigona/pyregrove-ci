# 1.0.0-alpha.30 — Edge to edge

- The game now fills wide phones. Instead of black bars at the sides, you
  see more of the forest and the caves, with the hearts, buttons and pause in
  the corners where your thumbs expect them. Prefer the old framing? Settings
  > Fill screen turns it off.
- The camera follows your knight more smoothly. It used to move the whole
  scene in small jumps, which made the hero look like he wobbled while
  running. It now steps by single screen pixels, so the pixel art stays crisp
  and the motion reads clean.
- The backdrop has a little more life: drifting embers in the forest levels
  and slow dust in the caves.
- Rootway Ruins: one of the hoppers used to start stuck inside the rock. It
  now waits on top of the ledge like it should.
- Your saves and settings carry over as they are.

Built for Android. No ads, plays offline. Optional anonymous analytics stay
off unless you turn them on.
