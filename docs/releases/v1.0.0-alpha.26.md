# 1.0.0-alpha.26 — A softer landing

- Forgiving jumps are on by default. A tap reaches full height; an early
  second tap saves your double jump for near the top instead of wasting it.
  Falling is gentler. Turn this off in Settings for the classic controls;
  the choice applies when you enter the next level.
- French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese for first-session menus,
  difficulty and comfort controls, pause/results actions and Forest Edge
  lessons. Device language is automatic; Settings has a manual choice.
  Items, later stories, legal notices and some summaries remain English.
- Pause and result panels scroll on short screens. Long translated result
  controls wrap instead of shrinking.

## VERIFIED engineering evidence — September 8

- Analyzer clean; 637 tests pass / 2 existing manual skips before the
  release-version rerun. Original assertions/checks preserved.
- Seven single-frame double taps, 50–350ms apart, reach more than four
  tiles. All 12 non-boss campaign/bonus exit-route simulations pass.
- Route bots replenish health to isolate geometry. This does not prove
  combat balance, every optional secret or all forgiving boss strategies.
- Translated title/settings, pause/results and sign drawing tested. No
  altered level IDs, save schema, telemetry consent or enemy physics.
