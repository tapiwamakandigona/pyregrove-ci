## 2. What the table actually says

1. **The Play cold-launch failure mode is universal, not ours.** Three
   independent 2022–2025 receipts (11 installs, 32 sales, 16 sales) reproduce
   Emberdelve's 38 almost exactly. Emberdelve was not an execution failure;
   it was the documented base rate.
2. **No modern Play-native receipt clears the bar.** The best one on record —
   pre-registration, localized screenshots, zero spend — produced ~900
   installs, and its author frames the win as "enough real players to get
   actual retention data", not as a business. The 2015-era receipts that do
   clear 1,000 depended on channels (mobile review press, editorial
   featuring) that are dead or lottery-shaped today.
3. **Where the modern channel receipts exist, they pay out on Steam.** Every
   2025–2026 no-budget success story in this hunt (4K, 10K, 28K wishlists)
   is short-form video converting to STEAM wishlists. None of them even
   route the traffic to Google Play. The audience-building channel in
   AUDIENCE-PLAYBOOK.md is real and proven — but the receipts say its natural
   destination for a platformer is a Steam page, not a Play listing.
4. **Short-form is a lottery with a grind floor, not a plan.** The same
   authors' non-viral videos got 200–1,000 views; the winners are outliers
   they could not predict (Ortica's hit was a repurposed old meme). It is a
   channel worth grinding for its floor, not a launch plan for its ceiling.

## 3. Consequences (the talking-us-out-of-things part)

- **Flip condition #1: NOT MET, and likely not meetable for Play.** If it
  stays unmet, LAUNCH-WORTHINESS.md's "not yet" hardens toward "not on Play
  at all" — which the freeze already enforces. No action needed; the freeze
  is now also evidence-backed, not only directive-backed.
- **If Pyregrove ever launches anywhere with a distribution story, the
  receipts point to short-form devlog → Steam wishlist page**, with Play (if
  at all) as a trailing port once an audience exists. A Flutter desktop/Steam
  build is a real technical question with a real answer, but investigating it
  is feature work — frozen, noted, not actioned.
- **The one Play-native tactic with a receipt — pre-registration — is
  compatible with the closed-track path** already recommended: it produces
  retention evidence from real players without spending the cold-start
  window on a public launch.
- What would change this verdict: a post-2023 documented case of a
  no-budget platformer clearing 1,000 first-month Play installs through a
  repeatable channel; or Pyregrove producing its own receipt via the
  audience playbook (which then satisfies condition #2 simultaneously).
